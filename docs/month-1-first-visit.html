Four jobs, one first visit

The path is already made: pick a profile, connect ads, confirm the brand, pick a plan, then a short tour and Orion. Now we count each step, watch our users, and fix what fails, with more design if we need it. Updates go in this group as notes, not as a demo call.

Job 1. Count the first visit

Screens will not tell us if we won, so first I write down how the path works today.

I list every step: sign up, pick a profile, connect ads, confirm the brand, pick a plan, start the tour, finish the tour, start Orion, finish Orion, send the first chat. Then I ask the engineers to record each of those steps in our analytics tool, which is the software that stores “this user finished Connect” without anyone watching. If analytics is not ready in the first week, I still run the user testing calls and write what each customer did in a Google Sheet, so we are not guessing.

The job is done when every step has a name we can count and we know today’s number, even if that number is bad. We will know by how many users start each step and how many finish, and how long it takes from sign up to Connect.

Job 2. Make setup easy

The screens are already there: profile, connect Meta, Google or Shopify, confirm the brand, pick a plan.

Sales asks five Flable customers to join a user testing call (about 30 minutes on Zoom or Google Meet). We can give credits or a referral code so they opt in. The customer shares their screen and clicks the new first visit while I watch, remote. I do not help unless they are stuck for a long time, and I write where they stop. If five calls are slow to book, I watch recordings of new signups instead, or sales asks three questions at the end of their next five calls. Then I change the words, the buttons, or the order, and I will do more design if that is what it takes. I will fix the three worst stuck places, which is often Connect feeling scary, a plan showing up before they have seen anything useful, or a step that never says why they are doing it.

The job is done when those three stuck places are fixed. We will know by how many users finish Connect, how long setup takes, and whether anyone writes to support and says they got stuck.

Job 3. They know what Orion is

The Orion questions are already there: who it works for, what to make better, where to push, how often to reach them, then the first chat.

A new Flable user should be able to say “Orion watches my ads and tells me what to do next.” They finish the short Orion questions or they ask Orion one real question, and if they cannot say that sentence we are not done, so I will change the words (and the screens if I must) until they can.

The job is done when four of the five customers on the calls can say what Orion is for in one sentence. We will know by how many users finish the Orion questions or send one chat, and I will write down the five answers they say out loud.

Job 4. They see the other parts of the app, and they know how to come back

The short tour is already there (sidebar, agents, the box where you type, partners, setup) and so is the setup list (personalize, connect, fix ad tracking, free credits).

Each stop on the tour must say the name of the thing and why it is there, so after the tour a user can point to one part of the app that is not chat. I will rewrite those stops and the setup list (personalize, connect, fix ad tracking, credits) so coming back is obvious. We do not grade “again and again” in these 22 days, we make it clear how to come back.

The job is done when four of the five customers on the calls can name one part of the app besides Orion chat. We will know by how many users finish the tour or open two parts of the app, and whether they started the setup list.

If a job finishes early I keep going, including more design (see the last page)

I will not send you extra screens unless they help one of these four jobs
