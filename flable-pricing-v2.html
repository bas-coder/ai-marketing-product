Choose your plan

Your plan decides which modules open in the sidebar. Credits are spent whenever Orion does the thinking: a chat, an AI Lens, a root cause, a custom report. Price scales with your ad spend, because that is what Flable is protecting.

What uses credits

Anything that asks Orion to reason costs credits, at a flat rate whatever the answer takes to produce. Reading a dashboard, opening a report or receiving an alert costs nothing.

Answers are cached for the day, so if a teammate already ran it you see the same result free. Top up any time: 1,500 for $10, 5,000 for $30. Unused credits roll over for a month.
