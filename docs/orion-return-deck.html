Idea · not a spec Writesonic-style switch

Do not override the live UI. Let people switch.

The new UI is in the prototype. The team has not fully taken it. I think that is fear of wiping the product existing users already live in. Fair. So we do not ask engineering to cut them over.

The problem

A hard cutover feels like a wipe.

Customers already know today’s shell. Shipping Orion as “everyone gets the new UI on Monday” is why the prototype stalls.

The move

A choice, with prompts.

Like Writesonic: ship the new UI, let users opt in. Prompt at high-intent moments — onboarding, a notification when Orion found something, and in the product (“this is easier here”).

What we are not asking

No cold cutover.

We respect the people already in Flable. We earn the switch. That is how we land Orion without burning the live UI.

flable.ai
What that means for design

If we prompt the switch, the new UI has to teach.

A nicer shell is not enough. People will try it, get lost, and go back. Then we will think the idea failed.

My job on the prototype

Guide them at every point.

Lots of educational content. What Orion is. What this module is. What to do next. Onboarding, notifications, and in-flow prompts that say why the new UI is better — not a cold empty frame.

The agreement I need

We educate in-product. We do not dump a new shell.

Engineering and design have to mean the same thing by “switch.” Prompt at moments the new experience is obviously better. If we cannot teach it, we should not prompt it.
